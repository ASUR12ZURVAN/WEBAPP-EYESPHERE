About EyeSphere

Vision Beyond Limits - Excellence in Eye Care

Our Mission

EyeSphere is where the pinnacle of vision care excellence converges with bespoke solutions for individuals and organizations. With cutting-edge diagnostic technology, experienced clinicians, and a compassionate approach, we are committed to providing comprehensive eye care that goes beyond treatment — empowering you to live life with clarity and confidence.

Leadership & Expertise

Dr. Mohita Sharma serves as Chief Ophthalmologist at Eyesphere Medicare and is recognized for her extensive expertise with over 35 years of experience in ophthalmology, particularly in diabetic retinopathy and retina procedures. She has performed advanced retina laser treatments and custom care plans leading to restored vision for patients with severe cases.

Alongside Dr. Sharma are key ophthalmology professionals including Dr. Robin Aggarwal – Lead Ophthalmic Surgeon, and Dr. Abhiyan Kumar Pattnaik – Senior Consultant.

Services & Offerings

Eyesphere Medicare delivers a wide array of eye-care services including General & Specialty Care (OPD, IPD, surgeries, opticals), Preventive & At-Home Services (vision therapy, dry eye treatments, low vision rehab), International Patient Services (teleconsultations, visa assistance, travel support), Corporate Wellness Programs, and Subscription-Based Support ("NETRAKAVACH").

  • Personalized treatment plans
  • Preventive screenings for long-term vision health
  • Professional and compassionate team
  • Cutting-edge technology and equipment
  • Comprehensive care for all ages
