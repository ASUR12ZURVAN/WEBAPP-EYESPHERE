Our Services

Comprehensive eye care solutions tailored to your unique needs

Vision Distance Screening

Vision distance screening, also known as a visual acuity test, measures how well you can see objects at a distance by having you read rows of letters on a Snellen chart from a specific distance, typically 10 feet or 3 meters.

Peripheral Vision Screening

Measures your ability to see objects outside your direct line of sight, assessing how far your eyes can see to the sides, up, and down while looking straight ahead.

Dry Eye Screening

Identify and measure level of dryness and recommend suitable treatment and management. Special dyes are used to highlight irregularities on the corneal surface caused by insufficient tears.

Color Blindness Screening

Standard tests to detect color vision deficiencies and advise on next steps. For severe cases, color blindness can impact the ability to distinguish traffic signals, making screening important for road safety.
